Remanufactured vs original: which to choose?

Remanufactured cartridges are original cartridge shells that are cleaned, refilled and tested. They are the best choice for everyday printing like documents, school work and shipping labels, where the savings matter most.

Original cartridges make sense for specialty printers and color-critical work, such as sublimation printing, or when a printer rejects third-party cartridges.

Either way, match the exact cartridge number from your old cartridge or your printer's manual before you order.

Tips to spend less on printing

Print in draft or eco mode for everyday documents.

Buy high-yield cartridges when they are available. They cost more upfront but less per page.

Keep a spare cartridge on hand for busy weeks, and store it sealed in a cool place.

For small offices, bundle orders for several printers at once to hit the free-shipping minimum.
